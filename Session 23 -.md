## Recap
**Much has happened** these past months, too much to tell now, but here is what is most important.
- After taking note of their surrounds from the vista provided from the battlements of [[Vraath Keep]], the group decided to make their way north towards a treehouse of sorts that they managed to identify.
- Having arrived at the treehouse, they came across a deceased elf and his journal, as well as a small cache of items that would prove useful on their journey. They buried the elf and after spending some time mining some partially lumanescent ore, they deciphered some information found within a map within his journal, namely a settlement in the hills east of Vraath keep, blood and death written about in the valley north of that, and a bridge between the two.
- After they finished preparations to go West through the forest, the group were ambushed by a hobgoblin regiment, which is where we return as they chase the remaining roblins through the underbrush.
