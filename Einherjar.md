># Einherjar Shades
![[Pasted image 20261001120503.png]]

The **Einherjar** were an army of paladins, barbarians and clerics of [[The Raven Queen]] devoted to [[Unnamed Avatar of the Raven Queen]] and the [[Valkuur]] during their reign. They brought death to the armies of demonkind.
