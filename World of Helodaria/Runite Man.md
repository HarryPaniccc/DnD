# Description
The creature has had lines of thick [[Runite]] traced along its form. A stake of runite, about 3/4 of a meter long, has been plunged through the creature's form. A trail of darkened stone and mold leads from one of the tables to where it lies.

Its eyes have been replaced with pools of once molten metal.

# Biography
**After Resurrection**
- He fled instantly
- Made his way towards [[Unnamed Hammlet Near the Synod of Elevation]]
- Ate a shit ton of winter stores and killed a man who went to investigate
- Disappeared towards the south. Last the villagers knew their hunters had lost track of him approaching the [[Ashen Peaks]].
