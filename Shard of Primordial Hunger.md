The Shard of Primodial Hunger is an artifact of immense power. During the [[Dawn War]], the [[Primordial of Hunger]] was slain and pieces of his essence were scattered through the world. The largest of his componenets, this shard, would inevitably be forged into the [[Vessel of Avarice]]. 

[[Kanan Einzbern]] would destroy the Vessel with the blood god [[Athelstan]] trapped within. Athelstan would fuse to the shard, his rage and will now becoming a part of it, though Athelstan's identity would be lost. All that exists is the hunger now.

The shard appears as a floating ring of gold shards orbiting a red, smoldering mass of animated wolf-like visages constantly devouring one another. Or devouring itself. The shard fits comfortably in someone's hand.

It is an essential ingredient in forging [[Nightfall, the Hand that Feeds]].
