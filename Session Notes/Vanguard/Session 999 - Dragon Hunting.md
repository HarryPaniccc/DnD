## Recap
**Much has happened** these past months, too much to tell now, but here is what is most important.
- In a search for arcane secrets hidden away in an ancient ruin known as the [[Synod of Elevation]], the [[Vanguard]] members [[Rylai Sweetsong|Rylai]], [[Dusk]] and [[Isilme Vanariel|Isilme]] awoke three magical beings that had been slumbering or otherwise imprisoned within the halls of the sorcerer sanctum: [[Ophelia the Sylph]], a tiny air elemental who takes the form of a miniature glowing blue girl; [[Maurice the Flameskull|Kran the Flameskull]], an acolyte of the ancient sorcerer empire who met a fate worse than death and is now serving under [[Caede]] in the [[Deathwatch]]; and a third, less kind and far more dangerous being.
- This [[Runite Man]], a person of massive statue who appeared to have large, deep, river-like traces of runite etched along his body, filling his eye sockets with deep pools of the metal, and a massive spike of the substance spearing his sternum, did a brief battle with the three warriors, and fled into the wilderness before he could be slain.
- Now, having returned from the hard part of their pilgrimmage to [[Steinwalde]] north of [[Sirkonos]], [[Caede]] and [[Kanan Einzbern]] have reunited with the rest of the Vanguard in Trayfort keep, collected their gear, and set out into the northern wilderness, hunting this creature, the effort led by Dusk and Caede.
- During the first days of their hunt, the group started their search in a marauder camp that the runite man had seemingly decimated, and where Caede and Dusk had slain the remaining marauder a night before. The Einzbern set to work, licking bootprints and sniffing the snow and whatnot, and they came across a corpse of a man who had been severed in two. 
- After Dusk experienced a slight, strange, uncanny hunger for the body of the deceased man, one that disgusted her, the group set off, following Kanan's grim psychometry and the intention it revealed.
- The group maintained their heading for some time, Caede receiving flashes of memories from his former life, and eventually it became clear that they were being persued by shades of fallen warriors. Caede recognised these as the spirits of soldiers that were loyal to the Valkuur, but they did not interact with the group, and maintained a distant following distance. 
- They now awaken the following day, Rylai and Dusk in Dularen at the [[Dripping Barrel Inn]] with the objective of purchasing spell components, and the remainder of the adventurers in a cave, sheltered from the wind and snow of the outside world.
