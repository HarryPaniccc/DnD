# Overland Travel
Travel pace is defined as 3 miles per hour with one more or one less mile per hour if the group is going fast or slow respectively. The following is used to adapt the rates:

## Travel Pace
As a baseline, pace is calculated as follows:
- **Normal**: A normal travel pace is the baseline movement overland. When crossing terrain that doesn't impede movement, one hex can be traversed in two hours.
- **Fast**: When the charaters are moving at a fast pace they travel 33% faster than usual. For unimpeded movement, this translates to an extra hex per day.
- **Slow**: The characters sacrifice speed for stealth, moving 33% slower, translating to a loss of one hex per day for unimpeded movement

## Modifiers
The primary cost to moving through a set of terrain includes things like the presence of a road, mounts, the types of terrain present, and the weather.

- **Roads**: For every two hexes travelled over a period of time, a road gives an additional hex of distance, and travelling on a road does not impede movement (unless it is reasonable for it to do so, such as after a calamity or something). For example, at a normal pace, a group travels 6 hexes in 8 hours
- **Mounts:** While riding on a mount, travel pace increases 50%, gaining an additional hex per two. Slower groups like trade caravans don't gain this benefit.
- **Terrain**: Different terrain types impede speed differently.
- **Weather**: Bad weather slows you by a quarter.

## Actions
One can spend time searching through a hex but rolls another random encounter.

#### 3 2 | Decrepit Mead Hall
**Boglands**
A ruined dwarf mead hall constructed from the wood of the surrounding forest lies partially buried in the bog. Holes in the ceiling and snow piled up inside in one of the vast corners. A great fireplace exists in the centre and there are unfathomably old casks of ale to be found. The grounds are haunted by dwarven ghosts and stalked by werewolves.

#### 4 1 | Stone Circle - End of the Line
**Heavy Dark Woods**
*This is where the [[Runite Man]] trail disappears.*
A large circle of stones on a dip in the land - like an inverted hill. There are carvings and runes along the stones that can be deciphered into high level spells.

#### 4 2 | Vampire Crypt
**Heavy Dark Woods**
Hidden beneath a messy waterfall lies an ancient stone tomb, overgrown with moss. There is a terrible stench coming from within.

Three Vampires were entombed within this structure, staked in their resting places and covered in steel in their coffins. Recently, a werewolf managed to get into one and devoured its heart, and has become a *Deathwolf*. The vampires have degenerated into *Nosferatus* in this time.


#### 4 4 | Ashen Legion Outpost
**Ashmounts**
On a sharp crag overlooking the north of the Ashmounts sits an [[Ashen Legion]] watchtower connected by a small, treacherous path back to [[Ralengrad]]. The posting is not well armed, and is more of a punishment than an honor, but is important to see incoming threats, and guards the safest passage over the mountains to this part of the world.
